# Local business and jobs

Details for `leitvia-seo-geo`. Read this for organization data, job postings, or before sections of a one-pager move to pages of their own.

- **Organization data:** while no SEO plugin is active, Leitvia Studio prints the organization as structured data on the front page only, and the news pages use the same source. Read the values with `leitvia/get-seo` and set them with `leitvia/set-organization`: `type` (a subtype of `LocalBusiness` from the list of the ability, `Organization` when none fits), `founding_date` (`YYYY`, `YYYY-MM` or `YYYY-MM-DD`), `area_served` (the towns and regions it serves), `opening_hours` (`days`, `opens`, `closes`), `contact_type` (what phone and email are for, such as `customer service`, the default, or `sales`), `same_as` (its profiles on other sites), `description` (one or two sentences on what it does) and `logo` (an image ID for structured data; search engines show it on white, so a light header logo can stay in the header), each from the person or the imprint, never guessed. With a phone or an email every type gets a `ContactPoint` whose `hoursAvailable` are the `opening_hours`; a local business also prints them as `openingHoursSpecification`. So service times of an `Organization` (a support line, an office without walk-in customers) go into `opening_hours` too, no switch to `LocalBusiness` needed. Only the fields you send change; each change waits in a package for a person. With an SEO plugin, list the values for the person to enter there.
- **Job postings:** Google reads `JobPosting` only on a page with one position, so each open position gets a page of its own, linked from a list of all positions. Leitvia Pro has a block per position that writes the data from its fields and the organization data; without it, an SEO plugin does. Set type, start, place and the end of the posting. A posting past its end shows as filled and loses its structured data: set a new end or remove the position, never keep an old date to stay in search. A full page cache can keep the old state until it expires. A salary only from the person.
- **Anchors of a one-pager:** links from other sites and search results point at sections such as `/#services`, and a redirect cannot catch them, since the browser never sends the part after `#`. Before sections of a single page move to pages of their own, list every anchor of the page (`leitvia/get-page-outline`, the `anchor` of each block in `leitvia/get-block`). Keep each old section with its anchor as a short teaser that links to the new page, and report every anchor with its new target. Replacing a pattern drops the anchors inside it.
