# Photo collage and gallery

Details for `leitvia-page`, read when a section shows several photos of people, trips or a group, or many photos of one service or project.

For sections about a person, a trip or a group with three to five photos of their own next to a short text, use `leitvia/content-photo-collage`: a gallery with the block style `collage` beside an eyebrow, a heading, a lead, a paragraph and a button. Give each image an `image` edit with its own alt text and `remove` the images you lack (three at least). On a page with several such sections alternate the side and the background (`surface`, `surface-alt`, a `surface-extra` of the token set). The photos go right with the block style Media right on the `core/columns` of the section: after the page exists, set `className` `is-style-media-right` on the columns with `leitvia/update-block` ([after the page exists](composition.md#after-the-page-exists)). Not for product shots or illustrations; for more than five photos use a gallery.

For six to about forty photos of one service, project or place, use `leitvia/content-gallery`: a heading, a lead and a `core/gallery` of eight photos in one 4:3 frame, four across from tablet width and two on phones, each opening whole in the lightbox. Add photos with `repeat` on the last image and `remove` the ones you lack; keep a multiple of four so the last row is full. Every photo gets its own alt text. One gallery per service; never a row of single image blocks or a section with one large photo per motif. A product next to its buy box takes the scroll gallery, three to five photos beside a text the collage.
