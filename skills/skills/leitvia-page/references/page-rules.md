# Page rules

Details for `leitvia-page`. Read this before you report a page as done.

- A block without server rendering keeps its content in its saved HTML. Use a pattern that holds it, or copy `example` from `leitvia/list-blocks`; a self-closing counter, bento, timeline, marquee, spotlight or reveal is rejected. Figures that count up come from `leitvia-studio/content-stats-counter`.
- A marquee of short statements (keywords, services) goes in with `insert` from its example, its list as `inner`: `{"block": "p:0.1", "insert": {"block": "leitvia/marquee", "inner": [{"block": "core/list", "inner": [{"block": "core/list-item", "text": "Strategy"}, {"block": "core/list-item", "text": "Press"}]}]}, "position": "after"}`. Type the words in normal case; the band stays still with reduced motion and has a Pause button. At most one per page, never for information people must read.
- A section keeps no sample text of its pattern: the write warns with `leitvia/sample-text` at each block that still holds one word for word. The citation of a quote (`leitvia/content-quote`) is the `text` of its `core/quote` block, not a block of its own: set it, or send `"text": ""` when the quote has no source.
- Steps and phases use `leitvia/content-steps`, with an icon and a title per step `leitvia-studio/content-process-icons` or the timeline `leitvia-studio/content-process-timeline` ([icon sections](icon-sections.md)), dated milestones and the history of a business `leitvia-studio/content-timeline` ([business pages](business.md)). Never build them from a list.
- Measure in the browser, not from the file: images meant to match, such as two portraits, must come out at the same rendered width and height. Compare `getBoundingClientRect()` at 1400 and 390 px.
- A row never leaves an orphan: four items in a row of three end as 3 + 1. Set `columns` 2 on the grid (two by two; with 4 a narrower window wraps to 3 + 1 again), or change the number of items with `remove` or `repeat`. A columns block has one column per `core/column`.
- Sections must differ visibly across the whole page, by tone, layout and image. A page of equal bands passes the score and still reads as one block: look at the full page before you report it.
- A dark footer counts as a neighbor: after a band on ink or inverse, Footer mega shows only a hairline as a seam, so close such pages on `surface` or `surface-alt`.
- A header with a button holds only so many menu items in one row: check it at 1024 px. When the items wrap into two lines, shorten the labels or group pages in a submenu; below 1024 px the items sit behind the menu button anyway. The bar of `leitvia/header-utility` keeps one line with its first link below 600 px, so put the most important contact first; the whole bar stands at the end of the menu.
- Headings hyphenate on their own in the language of the page: words from 12 letters, at least 5 on each side. Never put soft hyphens (`&shy;`) into a heading or a title; theme and Studio remove them from the document title, feeds, breadcrumbs and JSON-LD, but they stay in the stored title. A heading that still breaks badly gets shorter wording. Paragraphs, lists and table cells hyphenate as well.
- Where submenus open on click (the header patterns), the parent item is a button. The theme lists the page of the parent as the first entry of its own submenu, so do not add an overview link by hand. A parent without a page of its own has no address: leave out `url` of its item in `leitvia/update-navigation` (never `#`, which the link check refuses); it then only opens its submenu and gets no such entry.
- The default footer (pattern `leitvia/hidden-page-footer`) shows the site title, the copyright line, a link to the theme and the legal links the site has: the privacy policy page of WordPress, and an imprint or accessibility statement when a published page has the slug `impressum`, `imprint`, `legal-notice`, `barrierefreiheit` or `accessibility`. Give those pages these slugs and they appear by themselves. Every footer pattern also holds a Cookie settings link (class `leitvia-consent-link`, address `#leitvia-consent`): Leitvia Studio shows it on every page and opens its consent dialog with it, which says so when the site uses no cookies or services that need consent. Keep it when you edit a footer; without Studio the theme leaves it out. Further links come with a footer pattern and real targets (setup does that from the recipe); never keep sample links or `#`.
- Captions of a gallery stand below each image in the text color of the section, so they may be a full line. Only the style collage keeps them on the image: keep those to a few words.
- `align` `full` only at the top level of the content. Inside a column, details, tabs or a card in a grid a block cannot reach the edge of the window: leave its alignment out (theme 1.2.41 and later show it with the normal edge).
- A full-width band as the last section meets the footer on every page template, so no strip of page background stays under it. Its own padding gives the space. Two full-width bands with a ground of their own (background color, inverse, cover) touch on every page template as well (theme 1.2.50 and later); next to a section on the page ground the block gap stays.
- The button of a header stays reachable at every width, below 600 px at the end of the menu.
- The main button of the hero stays in the first screen at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844, also with breadcrumbs: keep heading and text within the `contentLength` of the pattern, or move the buttons above the text. `leitvia/check-composition` with `post` reports `firstScreen` (no points) only when the button really ends below the first screen: estimated from the lines of heading and text with the measured model of the stage (`docs.firstScreen`), the service bar of the header counts, the front page has no breadcrumbs. The message names the size and about how many pixels. The outline cuts texts at 80 characters and marks them with `text_truncated` and `text_length`; read the whole text with `leitvia/get-block` before you reuse it. Its `text` is the text the page shows: shortcodes such as a bound price resolved (only a `core/shortcode` block shows its shortcode), non-breaking spaces kept; the saved markup with the shortcode is in `leitvia/get-block`, so take `replace_text` from there.
- A service bar above the header (`header-utility`) keeps the class `leitvia-service-bar` and the phone link first in its right group: below 600 px the bar shows only that link on one line and repeats in full at the end of the menu.
- A section that belongs under every post, such as a closing call to action, goes once into the template part `post-closing`, not into each post: `leitvia/update-template-part` with the markup of the pattern as `content`. Empty, the part renders nothing.
- Blocks for every post go into template parts of the template single, never into each post: `post-after-content` right after the text, before tags, author box and related posts (a share block, a note, an ad slot), and `post-aside` at the end of the sidebar, which stands right of the text and sticks from 64rem and sits above the text on smaller screens (a fact box, an ad slot). Write them with `leitvia/update-template-part` and `content`; empty, they render nothing.
- Texts and choices of the single post are one setting, `leitvia/set-post-details`: the date labels of byline and sidebar (`updated_label` `"As of %s"`, `%s` is the date), the heading of the related posts (`related_heading` `"More from this category"`), their source (`related_by` `categories`, `primary` for the primary category of Yoast SEO or Rank Math, or `tags`) and the aspect ratio of the featured image (`image_ratio`, `auto` keeps each image as it is). A person applies it.
- Read a template or template part before changing it: `leitvia/get-template` with `template` (`single`, `archive`) or `part` returns its markup, outline, the patterns it places and the hash. A whole template changes with `leitvia/update-template` and a template pattern whose `templateTypes` hold the slug, or with the changed markup as `content`; keep the `core/pattern` blocks you leave as they are, so they follow updates of the theme. A single page or post takes another template with `template` of `leitvia/update-content`.
- Category and tag archives show the name of the term as the h1 (no "Category:" prefix), the chips of the categories (the twelve largest and the current one from thirteen on) and the description of the term as intro: [blog](blog.md).
- An embed of a provider Studio does not know shows a consent placeholder with its host name only: add the provider with `leitvia/set-consent-providers` (read `leitvia/get-consent-providers` first), facts from the provider.
- A newsletter sign-up is a newsletter block from `leitvia/list-blocks`, never a script, embed or form of the newsletter service; without such a block it is an open question.
